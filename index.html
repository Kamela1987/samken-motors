Vehicle collection & delivery service

Got a car stuck at the border or port?

Bought a vehicle yourself and it is stuck at a border or port with no one to drive it in? You do not have to be our customer — hire our experienced drivers to bring it home safely.

A car gets cleared, or nearly cleared, at Kazungula, Chirundu, Nakonde, Walvis Bay, Durban or Dar es Salaam, and then just sits there because there is no steady driver to bring it the rest of the way. Storage fees pile up while the owner waits, hundreds of kilometres away. Samken Motors supplies vetted, experienced drivers for exactly this job. Tell us where the vehicle is and how many you need collected, even 3 at once, and we will quote the job, assign a driver, and deliver it to you with photo updates along the way.

Frequently asked questions

Before you order

How does payment work?

You pay a deposit to confirm the order. We buy and ship the vehicle, and you pay the balance when it arrives in Zambia.

What is included in the quote?

Vehicle + transport + ZRA duty + clearing, in one written landed price.

Where do I send my payment?

Payments go to our business account only, and you receive a receipt for every payment.

Will I see my vehicle before paying the balance?

Yes. We send photo updates at every stage, and you inspect the vehicle before paying the balance.

Are the listed vehicles in Zambia?

Some are, and you can view them (the listing says where). Others are in South Africa and available to order, and we bring them to Zambia for you.

Can I get more photos or videos?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp and we will send more photos and videos of any vehicle.

Can you find a specific vehicle?

Yes. Tell us the make, model, year and your total budget, and we will send a written quote.

Why are some prices in kwacha and some in dollars?

Vehicle prices are in kwacha. The collection and delivery service for cars stuck at borders is quoted in US dollars because it is priced on cross-border corridor costs.
